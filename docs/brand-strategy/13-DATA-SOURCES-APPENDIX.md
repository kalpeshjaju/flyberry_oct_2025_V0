# FLYBERRY BRAND STRATEGY
## Section 13: Data Sources & Verification Appendix

---

## PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

**Why This Matters:**
Every claim in the Flyberry brand strategy must be defensible, verifiable, and current. This appendix documents:
- Data sources for all quantitative claims
- Verification methodology
- Last verified dates
- Evidence location (where proof documents are stored)
- Responsible parties for updates

**Principle**: "Trust but verify" applies to our own claims.

---

## VERIFICATION STATUS LEGEND

| Status | Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **VERIFIED** | ✅ | Data source documented, evidence on file, <6 months old |
| **PENDING** | 🔶 | Claim made but verification incomplete |
| **NEEDS UPDATE** | 🔄 | Evidence exists but >6 months old |
| **NO SOURCE** | ❌ | Claim lacks supporting evidence (MUST FIX) |

## SECTION 13: COMPLIANCE & LEGAL NOTES

### 13.1 Advertising Standards

**Relevant Regulations**:
- **ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India)** guidelines
- **FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India)** labeling regulations
- **Consumer Protection Act 2019** (misleading advertisement penalties)

**Key Principles**:
1. **Truthful**: Claims must be factually accurate
2. **Substantiated**: Must have evidence on file before making claim
3. **Current**: Data must not be outdated (define "current" per claim type)
4. **Not Misleading**: Context must not mislead even if technically true

**High-Risk Claims** (Require Strong Evidence):
- "Industry-first" (competitors can challenge)
- "Fortune 500" (must verify client is actually on Fortune 500 list)
- Nutritional claims (must comply with FSSAI RDA labeling rules)
- "Best-Seller" (must be verifiable on platform)

---

### 13.2 Intellectual Property Protection

**Defensible Claims to Trademark/Protect**:
- "Cold chain for dates" (if truly industry-first, consider trademark)
- Unique product names (e.g., "Majestic Medjoul")
- Packaging designs (design registration)

**Non-Defensible Claims**:
- Generic descriptors ("premium," "gourmet," "natural")
- Functional benefits already in public domain
