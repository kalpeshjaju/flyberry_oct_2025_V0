How to talk about each of our 8 hero products. Use these frameworks for product pages, sales sheets, social media, and customer conversations.

"The date that redefines what dates can taste like."

  • Melts with deep sweetness, caramel and toffee notes

Proof Points: 14% RDA magnesium, 9% potassium per 50g | Amazon best-seller 5+ years

Use When: Premium gifting, health-conscious snacking, natural energy

Ajwa Dates

"The blessed date from Medina—authenticity you can verify."

Australian Macadamia Nuts

"Buttery richness, heart-healthy fats—the macadamia as nature intended."

  • Style 0 premium whole kernels (top export grade)
  • 45.6% RDA of heart-healthy MUFA per 25g serving

Vacuum-Fried Chips (6 Varieties)

"Crunch without guilt—70% less oil, 100% flavor."

Key Differentiators:

  • 70% less oil absorption, nutrients and colors preserved

Proof Points: Industry-first in India | Taste the vegetable, not the oil

Use When: Healthy snacking, kids' lunch boxes, guilt-free indulgence

Competitive Positioning Messages

How to position Flyberry against key competitors without naming them directly (unless in B2B contexts where direct comparison is acceptable).

vs. Mass-Premium Brands (Happilo, etc.)

"Not all 'premium' is created equal."

Key Message:Many brands claim premium quality—Flyberry proves it. While others offer generic "Middle East dates," we specify Jordan Valley. While others have no Fortune 500 clients, we have 50+. While others use standard logistics, we pioneered cold chain for dates.

Proof Points:

  • Specific origin (Jordan Valley, Medina, Australia) vs. generic sourcing
  • Fortune 500 validation (10+ years) vs. no corporate proof
  • Industry-first innovations (cold chain, vacuum frying) vs. standard processing
  • QR-verified lab reports vs. unverified claims

When to Use: When prospects compare us to "other premium brands" or ask "What makes you different?"

vs. Limited Portfolio Specialists (Single-category brands)

"Complete excellence across 8 categories, not just one."

Key Message:Some brands excel in one category—Flyberry excels in eight. From dates to nuts to chips to spreads, every product represents #1 potential. One supplier for your entire premium portfolio, one quality standard across everything.

Proof Points:

  • 44 products across 8 categories (curated, not comprehensive)
  • Same #1 standard applied to dates as macadamias as chips
  • Simplifies procurement (one vendor, one quality bar)
  • Portfolio breadth drives basket size and revenue per customer

When to Use: B2B corporate/retail discussions where portfolio breadth matters

vs. Luxury Pricing (Bateel, etc.)

"Ultra-premium quality, accessible premium pricing."

Key Message:Luxury brands charge ₹2,000+ for dates—Flyberry delivers the same quality at ₹899. We don't compromise on sourcing (Jordan Valley Medjoul, Majestic grade), innovation (cold chain), or standards (Fortune 500 procurement). We just don't add luxury markup.

Proof Points:

  • Same origin quality (Jordan Valley Medjoul, Medina Ajwa)
  • Same grade selection (Majestic, Style 0)
  • Industry-first cold chain (luxury brands don't have)
  • ₹199-₹2,599 range vs. ₹2,000+ single-product pricing

When to Use: When prospects say "Too expensive" or compare to luxury brands

vs. Amazon Private Label / Generic Brands

"Fortune 500 companies choose brands, not commodities."

Key Message:Amazon private labels and generic brands compete on price—Flyberry competes on being the best. When Google needs executive gifts, they don't buy Amazon Basics. When Deloitte curates wellness programs, they choose verified quality. Brand matters when reputation is on the line.

Proof Points:

  • 50+ Fortune 500 corporate clients vs. zero for private labels
  • 10+ years brand equity vs. transactional commodity
  • Origin stories, QR codes, lab reports vs. generic packaging
  • Gifting-worthy presentation vs. functional commodity packaging

When to Use: B2B corporate gifting, when brand perception matters, executive/client gifts
