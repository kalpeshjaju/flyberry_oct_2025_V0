FLYBERRY POSM & RETAIL STRATEGY

Point of Sale Materials & In-Store Presence

1. POSM PHILOSOPHY & STRATEGIC APPROACH

Core Principle: "Retail presence reflects brand premium positioning"

Strategic Goals

  • Visual Distinction: Create clear differentiation on shelf vs. competitors
  • Brand Story Communication: Tell the origin and quality story at point of purchase
  • Impulse Purchase: Drive buying decisions through merchandising excellence
  • Staff Support: Educate retail staff with clear product knowledge materials
Non-Negotiables:

✓ All POSM must align with Visual Identity System (Section 11)
✓ Never compromise on material quality (cheap materials = cheap perception)
✓ Specificity over generic claims (show Jordan Valley, QR codes, Fortune 500)
✓ Consistency across all touchpoints (shelf, signage, sampling stations)

Cross-Reference: This strategy integrates with Section 11 (Visual Identity) and Section 2 (Brand Positioning) to ensure consistent brand presence.

2. STORE TYPE STRATEGY

A. Modern Trade (Supermarkets, Hypermarkets)

Shelf Space Strategy

  • Eye-level placement priority: Dates, nuts, and premium items at 4-5.5 ft height
  • Cross-merchandising: Dates near breakfast section, nuts near trail mix/health foods
  • Product clustering: Group by category (all dates together, all nuts together)
  • Secondary displays: Checkout counters for impulse purchases (single-serve packs)

Planogram Recommendations:

  • Horizontal blocking by product family (easier to shop)
  • Premium items (Medjoul, Macadamia) at center of block
  • Value packs on bottom shelf (bulk buyers will find them)

B. Specialty/Gourmet Stores

Premium Experience Strategy

  • Premium fixtures: Wooden or acrylic display units that reflect quality
  • Sampling stations: Always-on tasting areas with brand ambassador
  • Educational shelf-talkers: Origin stories, Fortune 500 validation
  • Staff training materials: Product knowledge sheets for recommendations

C. Traditional Trade (Kirana, Local Stores)

Space-Efficient Strategy

  • Simple displays: Counter-top units for limited space
  • Clear signage: Fewer products, bolder messaging
  • Small-format sampling: Single-serve packs for trials
  • Price communication: Visible pricing to reduce inquiry burden on store owner

3. SIGNAGE & DISPLAY SPECIFICATIONS

A. Shelf-Talkers (Essential)

Design Specifications

Size: 3" x 5" (vertical), 5" x 3" (horizontal)

Content Template:

[Product Hero Image] [Product Name - Bold Typography] [1-Line Taste Description] [Origin Badge: "Jordan Valley" or "Australian Coast"] [Fortune 500 Badge or QR Code]

Color Palette: Flyberry primary brown (#8B5A3C) + cream background (#F8F5F0)

Print Specs: 300 DPI minimum, coated cardstock, matte finish

Mounting: Magnetic or clip-on attachments (no tape that damages shelves)

B. Danglers & Wobblers (Seasonal/Campaign)

Campaign Signage

Use Case: Product launches, seasonal offers (Diwali, New Year, Summer)

Size: 6" x 8" double-sided

Design Rules:

  • Front: Campaign message ("New: Date Syrup" or "Diwali Gifting Collection")
  • Back: Flyberry brand mark + QR code
  • Text limit: Maximum 8 words for readability

Placement: Hang 12-18" above product for visibility without obstruction

C. Counter Cards & Tent Cards

Checkout Point Materials

Size: 4" x 6" folded tent card

Content: Best-sellers messaging ("Most Loved: Medjoul Dates" or "Try Our Date Syrup")

Placement: Checkout counters, sampling stations, inquiry desks

D. Posters (Large Format)

Feature Display Posters

Size: 18" x 24" (A2 standard)

Use Case: End-aisle displays, entrance features, in-store promotions

Visual Hierarchy:

  1. Hero product image (60% of space) - high-quality photography
  2. Tagline ("Extraordinary taste through extraordinary sourcing")
  3. Call to action ("Scan QR for origin story" or "Ask staff for tasting")

Material: Foam board for rigidity, weather-resistant coating for outdoor/cooler placement

4. SAMPLING STATION PROTOCOL

A. Setup Requirements

  • Branded table cover: Flyberry cream/brown colors, clean and wrinkle-free
  • Product display: Unopened packs (for trust) + open sample bowls
  • Staff presentation: Branded apron or t-shirt (if applicable)
  • Hygiene essentials: Hand sanitizer, disposable gloves, napkins, waste bin
  • Signage: "Taste the Difference" table tent with QR code

B. Sampling Script Template

GREETING: "Hi! Would you like to try our [Product Name]?" TASTE FIRST: [Offer sample without explaining] AFTER THEY TASTE: "What do you notice?" [Listen for their response - texture, sweetness, freshness] THEN EXPLAIN: "[Product] comes from [Specific Origin: Jordan Valley/Australian Coast]. We [Unique Process: cold chain/vacuum-fried/7-year maturation]. That's why it tastes like this." PROOF: "Fortune 500 companies like Google choose this for executive gifting. Scan the QR code to see the lab report." CLOSE: "Would you like to take a pack home?"

C. Do's and Don'ts

✅ DO

  • Let taste speak first (don't over-explain before they try)
  • Use sensory language ("soft," "buttery," "caramel notes")
  • Have product knowledge ready (origin, grade, Fortune 500 clients)
  • Keep sampling area clean and restocked every 2 hours

❌ DON'T

  • Start with health claims ("It's healthy for you")
  • Use generic adjectives ("delicious," "tasty," "amazing")
  • Push sales aggressively after sampling
  • Let samples sit out too long (replace every 2 hours)

5. BRANDED FIXTURES & DISPLAYS

A. Premium Display Unit Specifications

For Dedicated Flyberry Space

Type: Free-standing display rack (wooden or acrylic)

Dimensions: 36" H x 24" W x 12" D (holds 40-50 products)

Design Elements:

  • Top header: Flyberry logo + brand tagline
  • Shelf structure: 4 shelves with clear category separation
    • Top: Premium dates (Medjoul, Ajwa)
    • 2nd: Premium nuts (Macadamia, Pecan, Brazil)
    • 3rd: Specialty items (Date Syrup, Hazelnut Spread, Chips)
    • Bottom: Berries, trail mixes, bulk packs
  • Bottom shelf: Branded brochures, QR code cards, product catalogs

Material: Sustainable wood or matte-black metal (no cheap plastic - maintains premium perception)

Optional: LED strip lighting for premium stores (highlights products)

B. Shelf Organizers (Budget-Friendly)

For Shared Shelf Space

Type: Acrylic dividers or cardboard standees

Purpose: Create visual blocks for Flyberry products on shared shelves

Design: Minimalist with Flyberry logo + product category label

Benefit: Low-cost way to create "owned space" perception

7. MEASUREMENT & ROI TRACKING

Key Performance Metrics

Sales Lift: Target +15-25% during POSM campaigns vs. baseline

Track weekly sales data before, during, and after POSM deployment

Sampling Conversion: Samples given → purchases made

Target: 20-30% conversion rate (track with tally sheets)

QR Code Scans: Trackable via analytics on signage QR codes

Target: 5-10% scan rate from shelf-talkers

Store Staff Feedback: Quarterly surveys

Questions: Ease of setup, customer response, material quality

Monthly Review Protocol

  • Week 1: Audit POSM placement across all stores
  • Week 2: Replace damaged or outdated materials
  • Week 3: Analyze sales data vs. POSM investment
  • Week 4: Iterate design/messaging based on performance

ROI Calculation: (Sales Lift - POSM Cost) / POSM Cost × 100 = ROI %

Target ROI: Minimum 200% (₹2 sales lift for every ₹1 spent on POSM)
