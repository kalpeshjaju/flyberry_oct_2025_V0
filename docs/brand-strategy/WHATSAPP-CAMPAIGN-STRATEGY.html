FLYBERRY WHATSAPP CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

Section 19: Personal, Retention-Focused Communication

1. WhatsApp Philosophy & Strategic Approach

Core Principle

"WhatsApp is where brand becomes relationship. Not broadcast, but conversation."

Strategic Goals

  • Retention: Keep customers engaged between purchases
  • Personalization: 1-on-1 connection with high-value customers
  • Community: Build VIP circles and wellness groups
  • Service: Provide responsive, premium support experience
  • Cross-channel: Bridge Instagram discovery to WhatsApp loyalty

Non-Negotiables

  1. Respect privacy: Never spam, always opt-in
  2. Response speed: <2 hours during business hours, <24 hours always
  3. Tone consistency: Warm, personal, never robotic (see Brand Communication guide)
  4. Value first: Every message must provide value (offer, info, support)
  5. Integration: Link to larger customer journey (website, Instagram, retail)

WhatsApp Types We Use

  • 1-on-1 Messaging: Order updates, customer service, VIP engagement
  • Broadcast Lists: Exclusive offers, product launches (segmented by persona)
  • Groups: Wellness communities, corporate account managers, recipe circles

  • Business API: For scale (>1,000 customers), automated + human hybrid

B. API Features We Use

Automated Order Updates

Triggered by order status changes (confirmed → shipped → delivered)

Chatbot for FAQs

Handles common questions (delivery time, return policy, product info)

Human Handoff

Complex inquiries escalate to team member automatically

Broadcast Segmentation

Send different messages to different customer segments (VIPs, lapsed, corporate)

Analytics

Track message delivery, read rates, response times

5. Response Protocols & SLAs

Service level standards

A. Response Time Targets

  • Simple Inquiries: <1 hour (business hours)
  • Order Issues: <30 minutes (critical)
  • General Questions: <2 hours (business hours)
  • After Hours: <24 hours (next business day)

B. Escalation Protocol

  1. Level 1 (Chatbot): FAQs, order tracking, basic info
  2. Level 2 (Customer Service Team): Product inquiries, order modifications
  3. Level 3 (Manager): Complaints, bulk orders, partnership inquiries

C. Quality Standards

✅ Always Do

  • Address customer by name
  • Acknowledge their message before solving
  • Provide specific answers (not generic "we'll look into it")
  • Set clear expectations (timeline, next steps)
  • Close with gratitude and open door

❌ Never Do

  • Say "as per policy" (explain in human terms)
  • Deflect blame (own the issue, solve it)
  • Leave customer waiting without updates
  • Use robotic corporate language
  • Close conversation without resolution
