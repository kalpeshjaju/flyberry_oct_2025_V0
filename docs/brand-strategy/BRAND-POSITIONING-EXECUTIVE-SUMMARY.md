```
TASTE (40%)     →  What customers experience first
    ↓

    ↓
PROOF (25%)     →  Validation for those who want it
```

People who won't settle for mediocre. They've been burned by "premium" that wasn't.

**They ask:** "How does this taste?" before "What's the origin?"

**They value:**
- Soft dates over dried-out dates (texture matters)
- Buttery crunch over stale nuts (freshness matters)
- Vegetable taste over grease (quality matters)
