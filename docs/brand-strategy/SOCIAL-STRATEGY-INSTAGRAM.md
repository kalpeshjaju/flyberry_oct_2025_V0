# FLYBERRY SOCIAL STRATEGY
## Social Strategy - Instagram

---

## CONTENT GENRE 1: IN-STORE CONVERSATIONAL CONTENT

### One-Line Definition
A short-form series about the quiet mental thoughts/behaviors people have with food — told with humor, honesty, and zero judgment.

### Core Format
- **Constant anchor:** Store manager/store staff with a consistent tone and style
- **Rotating visitors:** Orri-types, creators, funny creators, Gen-Z, rich kids, know-it-alls, skeptical Gen-Z, etc.
- **Stage:** The Flyberry store counter is the recurring set — like a cafe in a sitcom

### Audience & Purpose
**Audience:** Founders, Marketing, Social Media, Content Team, ISB Interns

**Purpose:** Align the entire team on what kind of content we are building, why, and how to execute it consistently.

---

## 1. THE REAL GOAL (IN SIMPLE WORDS)

We are not trying to make Flyberry popular on Instagram. We are trying to make Flyberry worth following.

Brands like Mokai and TrueDiamond are followed because:
- People enjoy their content even when they are not buying
- Their content feels like a series, not ads
- They show how people think, not what products do

That is the level we want Flyberry to operate at.

---

## 2. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE (FOR EVERYONE)

**If the content is not interesting without Flyberry in it, it is not good content.**

Flyberry should appear:
- Naturally
- Casually
- As part of life

Flyberry should NOT:
- Explain itself
- Teach lessons
- Claim benefits
- Act as the hero

This is exactly how Mokai and TrueDiamond behave.

---

## 3. THE BIG INSIGHT (WHAT WE ARE REALLY SHOWING)

People don't follow content because of situations alone. They follow content because they recognize ways of thinking.

**Example:**
- Mokai shows different types of people in cafes
- TrueDiamond shows different types of thinkers in relationships
- Flyberry must do the same — but around food and snacking

---

## 4. FLYBERRY'S "SCHOOLS OF THOUGHT"

*(These are thinking styles, not target audiences.)*

These are the mental models people carry when it comes to food:
- "I eat whatever, deal with it later"
- "I plan snacks like an adult"
- "I want good taste but hate regret"
- "I don't trust packaged food easily"
- "I snack emotionally"
- "I snack practically"

**Important:** None of these are good or bad. We are not judging or correcting them.

---

## 5. HOW A SCHOOL OF THOUGHT BECOMES CONTENT

A mindset alone is boring. It becomes content only when it collides with real life.

**Every piece of content must have 4 parts:**

1. **Situation:** A real, familiar moment
2. **School of thought:** How someone thinks in that moment
3. **Collision:** Something or someone challenges it
4. **Human resolution:** Ironic, awkward, funny, or quietly satisfying

No preaching. No moral ending.

---

## REFERENCE REEL SCRIPT (IG-NATIVE)

**Series:** Flyberry Counter Conversations
**Episode:** "Marketing Clean"
**Length:** 18–22s
**Mental model:** I don't trust packaged food easily
**Visitor:** SOBO skeptic (stylish, self-aware, a little anxious about losing control)
**Anchor:** Manager (calm, playful, wins kindly — never judgy)

### Script Beats (Shootable)

**0:00–0:02** — Door chime. She walks in with sunglasses still on.
ON-SCREEN (big): "MARKETING CLEAN?"
**SOBO GIRL** (quick): "Before I try anything… are you clean clean… or marketing clean?"

**0:02–0:04** — Manager smiles, easy.
ON-SCREEN (small): "caught 😭"
**MANAGER:** "Same question for you— are you actually hungry… or just curious and dangerous?"

**0:04–0:08** — She grabs a pack, flips it instantly, scanning like a lawyer.
ON-SCREEN: "EXPERIENCE, NOT PARANOIA."
**SOBO GIRL:** "I've been lied to by fonts. 'No added sugar' always has… a plot."
**MANAGER** (warm): "That's not paranoia. That's experience."

**0:08–0:12** — Manager slides a tiny sample cup forward. No pitch.
**MANAGER:** "Don't trust the label. Don't trust me. Just take one bite."
**SOBO GIRL** (dead serious): "This is how I lose control."
**MANAGER** (gentle): "No—this is how you stop negotiating."

**0:12–0:16** — She takes a tiny bite. Neutral face… then a micro pause. She looks away like she got exposed.
**MANAGER** (soft): "Yeah… that pause was honest."
**SOBO GIRL** (defensive): "It's fine."

**0:16–0:19** — She freezes—hand already moving for bite #2.
ON-SCREEN: "hand betrayed her"
**MANAGER** (deadpan): "Fine doesn't go in for a second bite."

**0:19–0:22** — She smiles, gives up. Manager hands the bag.
END CARD (optional): "THE BITE ALWAYS WINS."
**SOBO GIRL** (smiling): "One pack. But I'm not endorsing."
**MANAGER:** "Perfect. Just… come back when you want to pretend you're 'just looking' again."

### Caption (Rewritten)

She walked in to test the label.
Left because the bite tested her.

## CONTENT GENRE 2: "TAKING FLYBERRY TO PLACES"

**Tagline:** Share a Flyberry.

### 1. THE CORE IDEA

Flyberry isn't just a snack you eat at home.
It's something you carry into moments — art, music, travel, work, celebration, chaos.

This genre documents Flyberry entering real-life environments and becoming part of the moment — quietly, naturally, without announcement.

**The place/event is the hero.**
Flyberry is the constant companion.

---

### 2. WHY THIS GENRE EXISTS

We are not creating content about Flyberry.
We are showing where Flyberry belongs in real life.

This genre:
- Makes Flyberry feel present in culture, not promotional
- Positions Flyberry as a shareable object, not a "product"
- Builds repetition and recognition without feeling like ads
- Scales across creators, customers, founders, team members

---

### 3. WHAT THIS GENRE SHOULD FEEL LIKE

**Tone:**
- Observational
- Casual
- Warm
- Slightly playful
- Never salesy

**Energy:**
- "I brought this along"
- Not "I'm promoting this"
- Flyberry appears the way coffee, notebooks, or headphones do — naturally

---

### 4. THE REPEATABLE FORMAT (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Every piece in this genre follows the same underlying structure.

**FORMAT FLOW:**

1. **Opening line (on-screen):**
   Taking Flyberry to ____

2. **Glimpse highlight:**
   Quick, strong visuals of the place or event (the vibe comes first)

3. **Flyberry cameo:**
   The pack appears naturally — in hand, on a table, in a bag

4. **Share moment:**
   Offering it, opening it, splitting it, or placing it for someone

5. **Closing line (on-screen):**
   Share a Flyberry.

---

### 5. CONTENT LENGTH RULES

**Snipped View (8–15s)**
When the place/event is self-explanatory (music gigs, standup, office, college fest)

**Longer View (20–45s)**
When the place/event itself is the story (travel, home tours, behind-the-scenes environments)

Length is decided by context richness, not algorithm pressure.

---

### 6. SIGNATURE VISUAL RULES (THIS MAKES IT A GENRE)

To ensure recognizability across posts:

**Must-have:**
- Flyberry pack held clearly at least once
- One quick selfie-style frame holding Flyberry
- Natural light whenever possible
- Real ambient sound from the place

**Avoid:**
- Studio lighting
- Over-framed product shots
- Influencer-style "presenting to camera"
- Forced reactions or testimonials

Flyberry should feel present, not pitched.

---

### 7. "SHARE A FLYBERRY" — THE CORE ACTION

This is the emotional center of the genre.

**Ways to execute:**
- Offering someone a piece
- Opening a pack and placing it in a shared space
- Handing it to a friend mid-moment
- Leaving it behind for someone else
- Silent acknowledgment: pack placed between people

Sharing can be explicit or implied — but it must exist.

---

### 8. PLACE / CONTEXT BUCKETS

#### 1. Art Places
**Angle:** Slow moments, culture, observation
**Scenes:** Galleries, exhibitions, installations
**Energy:** Quiet, thoughtful
**Caption cue:** "Some places ask you to slow down."

#### 2. Music
**Angle:** Energy, sound, crowd, backstage
**Scenes:** Gigs, jam sessions, rehearsals
**Energy:** Alive, immersive
**Caption cue:** "This moment needed a crunch."

#### 3. Stand-Up Comedy
**Angle:** Before/after laughter
**Scenes:** Green rooms, seating, post-show moments
**Energy:** Light, relaxed
**Caption cue:** "Laughs hit better with a break."

#### 4. Travel
**Angle:** Movement, transitions, waiting
**Scenes:** Airports, trains, road trips, hotel rooms
**Energy:** Companion-like presence
**Caption cue:** "Some things travel well."

#### 5. Home Tours (Customers)
**Angle:** Belonging
**Scenes:** Kitchens, desks, snack shelves
**Energy:** Intimate, real
**Caption cue:** "Where Flyberry fits at home."

#### 6. College Fests
**Angle:** Chaos, energy, sharing
**Scenes:** Crowds, stalls, friend groups
**Energy:** Fast, youthful
**Caption cue:** "Built for long days."

#### 7. Corporate Customers
**Angle:** Everyday work life
**Scenes:** Desks, meetings, coffee breaks
**Energy:** Calm, modern
**Caption cue:** "Good work needs good breaks."

## CONTENT GENRE 3: TRUST-BUILDING CONTENT

### Founder Talking About the Product

#### WHY THIS EXISTS

Flyberry believes its products are best-in-class.
This content exists to earn trust through clarity, not hype.

Not ads.
Not influencers.
Just the founder explaining things most brands won't.

---

#### CORE PRINCIPLE

**Authority through honesty.**
Specifics > buzzwords.
Trade-offs > tall claims.

---

#### WHO & TONE

**Founder on camera**
Calm, confident, slightly opinionated
Explaining, not selling.

---

#### WHAT TO TALK ABOUT

- Why this product exists
- What most brands hide
- How to judge real quality
- What Flyberry refused to compromise on
- Founder POV on taste & ingredients

---

#### FORMAT RULES

- 30–60 sec
- One idea per video
- Minimal edits
- Natural light / store / backroom
- No "buy now" CTA

---

#### LANGUAGE RULES

**Do:** Simple words, real facts, honest nuance
**Don't:** Luxury buzzwords, overclaims, sales talk

---

#### SUCCESS =

Saves, DMs, comments like:
- "This makes sense."
- "I trust this brand."

Not conversions.

---

#### LITMUS TEST

**"If this video existed without Flyberry branding—would it still be valuable?"**

If yes → post.

## CONTENT GENRE 4: FLYBERRY LIFESTYLE CONTENT

*(Inspired by café-style lifestyle feeds like Morii, adapted for Flyberry)*

### 1. What This Genre Is

Flyberry lifestyle content is not food photography.
It is life, with food quietly present.

We are not showcasing products.
We are showcasing a state of living where Flyberry belongs naturally.

**Think:**
"This is what good taste looks like in everyday life."

---

### 2. The Core Principle (Non-Negotiable)

**Food is the setting, not the subject.**

- The moment matters more than the product
- Emotion > aesthetics
- Context > close-ups

If the product were removed, the moment should still feel complete.

---

### 3. Structural Patterns That Make This Work

#### A. In-Between Moments (≈70%)

- Mid-bite
- Half-eaten food
- Hands reaching, passing, serving
- Laughter, pauses, mess

These moments feel observed, not staged.

---

#### B. People Without Performance

- Faces optional
- Hands, backs, profiles preferred
- No posing
- No "holding product to camera"

This creates universality — anyone can see themselves there.

---

#### C. Imperfection = Trust

- Slight blur
- Uneven framing
- Natural light
- Real tables, real homes, real settings

**Perfect = ad**
**Imperfect = life**

---

### 4. Flyberry Lifestyle Translation

Flyberry lifestyle should feel:
- Calm, not social-heavy
- Personal, not public
- Intentional, not aspirational

This is about how good food fits quietly into daily life — homes, desks, travel, gifting, pauses.

---

### 5. Flyberry Lifestyle Content Buckets

#### 1️⃣ Everyday Good Taste

Flyberry casually present in:
- Work desks
- Kitchen counters
- Car dashboards
- Pantry shelves

No explanation. No selling.

---

#### 2️⃣ Quiet Rituals

- Evening snack bowls
- Refilling jars
- Packing for travel
- Preparing gifts at home

Signals intentional living.

---

#### 3️⃣ Indian, Modern, Real

- Indian homes
- Indian light
- Familiar textures (steel, marble, wood)
- No fake Western minimalism

This grounded familiarity is a Flyberry advantage.

---

#### 4️⃣ Soft Social Moments

- Sharing food
- Conversations mid-meal
- Passing bowls
- Group energy without posing

Observed, not orchestrated.

---

### 6. What This Content Is NOT

- ❌ Product hero shots
- ❌ Health preaching
- ❌ Loud luxury
- ❌ Gym / protein framing
- ❌ Influencers talking to camera
- ❌ "Benefits of dry fruits" posts

Flyberry earns premium through calm confidence, not claims.

---

### 7. Caption Rules (Simple)

**Short**
**Thought-like**
**Never explanatory**

**Examples:**
- "Always runs out first."
- "Some habits don't need reminders."
- "Good food, no noise."
- "Keep something good around."
