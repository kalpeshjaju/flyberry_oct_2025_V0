## 1. PRICING STRATEGY

### The 4-Tier Architecture

**Philosophy:** 
Price reflects value, not cost. Fortune 500 validation justifies premium. Each tier serves a specific purchase occasion.

| Tier | Name | Price Range | Purpose | Products |
|------|------|-------------|---------|----------|
| **1** | Essential | ₹49-₹399 | Trial, impulse | Chips (₹49), single packs, small sizes |
| **2** | Signature | ₹399-₹999 | Regular purchase | 250g nuts, 500g dates, berries |
| **3** | Reserve | ₹999-₹2,599 | Premium gifting | Large packs, exotic nuts, gift boxes |
| **4** | Atelier | ₹2,599-₹7,249 | Luxury/Corporate | Custom hampers, Fortune 500 gifts |

## 2. PACKAGING STRATEGY

### Pack Size Logic

**Philosophy:**
Different pack sizes serve different occasions. Every size has a strategic purpose.

| Size Tier | Weight | Purpose | Target Persona |
|-----------|--------|---------|----------------|
| **Single Serve** | 20-25g | Impulse, trial, on-the-go | Rahul (millennial) |
| **Trial** | 100g | First purchase, testing | All personas |
| **Standard** | 250g | Regular consumption | Rahul, Meera |
| **Family** | 500g | Household staple | Ananya, Meera |
| **Bulk** | 1000g | Heavy users, value | All personas |
| **Gift Box** | Multi-product | Gifting occasion | Priya, Ananya |

---

### Packaging Design Specifications

**Primary Elements:**

| Element | Specification | Purpose |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| **Logo** | FLYBERRY GOURMET, Playfair Display Bold | Brand recognition |
| **Product Name** | Montserrat SemiBold, category color | Product identification |
| **Origin Badge** | Country flag + name | Sourcing story |
| **Grade Badge** | "Majestic", "Style 0" where relevant | Premium signal |
| **Nutrition Panel** | Full RDA percentages | Trust, transparency |
| **QR Code** | Links to lab reports + origin story | Verification |
| **Cold Chain Logo** | For dates | Innovation signal |

### Communication Hierarchy by Channel

**Website:**
1. Fortune 500 trust (hero section)
2. Product portfolio (categories)
3. Origin stories (sourcing)
4. Innovation (cold chain, technology)
5. Shop now (conversion)

**Instagram:**
1. Visual product shots
2. Lifestyle integration
3. Recipe content
4. Origin stories
5. Customer features

**Amazon:**
1. Product benefits (bullet points)
2. Nutritional data (RDA)
3. Origin/grade (in title)
4. Customer reviews
5. A+ content (brand story)

**Corporate Pitch:**
1. Fortune 500 client list
2. Quality assurance (cold chain, testing)
3. Customization options
4. Reliability track record
5. Volume pricing

## 5. IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)
- [ ] Finalize visual identity system
- [ ] Update packaging across top 15 SKUs
- [ ] Launch refreshed website
- [ ] Train sales team on brand narrative
- [ ] Create corporate presentation deck

### Phase 2: Activation (Months 4-6)
- [ ] Roll out new packaging to full portfolio
- [ ] Launch "Fortune 500 Trusted" campaign
- [ ] Implement tiered pricing strategy
- [ ] Expand quick commerce presence
- [ ] Execute first influencer partnerships

### Phase 3: Amplification (Months 7-12)
- [ ] Scale influencer program
- [ ] Launch corporate gifting microsite
- [ ] Introduce subscription options
- [ ] Expand to new retail partners
- [ ] Measure and optimize based on data
